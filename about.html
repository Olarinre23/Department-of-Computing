About Our Department

Who We Are

The Department of Computing is dedicated to providing students with the knowledge and skills required to succeed in the rapidly growing world of technology. We encourage creativity, teamwork and problem-solving.

Our Vision

To become a leading department in computing education, technological innovation and practical research.

Our Mission

To equip students with relevant computing knowledge, professional skills and the ability to develop technological solutions for society.

Our Values

Excellence, integrity, creativity, discipline, teamwork and continuous learning.
